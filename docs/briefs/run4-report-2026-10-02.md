## Benches

### The family-contract instrument, and why no `drops` moved

`server/bench/familyContracts.ts`. A real normal node of the family's own biome and tier
has its blend overwritten in-process to `{family: 1}` — one private host per family, since
`nodeSpawnBlend` memoizes per node id, which makes the override structural rather than
call-ordered. Spawner, density, pack logic, terrain and repopulation stay live; only
composition is forced, and nothing is written to disk. **This sidesteps the blend problem
by construction, which is its advantage over the matrix.** Fixed N = 30 kills per cell per
seed, 600 s cap, four seeds, measured against the band's own lane-less control and pooled
across every weapon lineage carrying the lane.

Five minimum-sample guards, symmetric in both directions, all of which must pass for
"decisive": **G1** the lane cell *and* its control each reach N, every seed, every carrier
(not "enough kills" but *the same* kills); **G2** carriers exist, counted; **G3** every
seed agrees on the sign; **G4** |mean margin| exceeds half the seed spread, so the interval
excludes zero — **the guard run 3 lacked**; **G5** the raw TTK-N ratio agrees in sign with
the control-normalised margin.

**No `drops` field moved, and that is the measured answer.** The instrument found exactly
one decisive inversion — **Cave Brute T1**, −6.4pp, all four seeds negative, all five
guards passing, with a legible mechanism (Affliction's payoffs firing 889–1499 times per
cell against Empowered's 60–120 on one 250 HP body, with `dotResistance: 0.30` already on
the family, so the punishment exists and is not enough). It then did not survive being
measured on a second node.

Measured across all five Cave T1 hosts at identical forced composition:

```
fav rank per host   6/7, 4/7, 1/7, 1/7, 4/7
per-host margin     -1.9%, -6.4%, +2.9%, +3.8%, -1.5%
pooled margin       -0.6%  against a host half-spread of +5.1%  -> UNDECIDED
pooled lane means   tempo +5.4, affliction +5.0, EMPOWERED +4.3, control +3.8,
                    kill-chain +3.3, engagement +1.9, technique +0.0
```

design never anticipated. Consequently **all sixteen decisive verdicts from the sweep — 12

`FAM_HOSTS_ALL=1` is the mode a `drops` decision has to be taken in, and the report marks a
pooled margin smaller than its host spread `UNDECIDED however clean each individual host
looked`. Re-measuring the sixteen is ~16 × 13 min: an overnight run of its own.

**Seven findings on this run's diff. Four fixed (`b28268e4`), each with a test verified to
fail without the fix. Two are design calls, escalated below. One was overstated.**

1. **`technique-refund` refunded nothing on three of four Technique shapes.** FIXED. The
   refund takes its share off what is **left** of a cooldown, but `instant`, `self-cast`
   and `cast` all resolved *before* `startTechniqueCooldown`, so the card read zero and —
   correctly — drew nothing. Slam, Snipe, Stunning Strike, Detonate and the Broadsword's
   `Set and Reset` were inert. Only the `armed` shapes worked, **which is exactly what the
   bench's reference wiring attunes**, so nothing measured it and W1-G's own tests passed:
   they called the seam directly with a pre-set cooldown rather than driving a cast. The new
   test goes through `beginAbilityCast` + `updateAbilityCasts`, and on the old ordering it
   fails at "a resolved cast Technique should fire the card once" — the card does not even
   register.
2. **The `evade` trigger was unreachable.** FIXED. W1-E's finding-5 fix gated it on
   `evadeMitigation >= 1`, but `EVADE_MITIGATION_BASE` is 0.5 and the best authored sources
   add +0.1 and +0.3, so full avoidance **cannot happen in authored content** and every
   `evade` card in the game went inert. The trigger now reads the successful dodge roll;
   `evadeBlocksDebuffs` keeps its own full-dodge-only standard, which is the rule W1-E was
   actually protecting. W1-E's test was rewritten to assert the distinction rather than
   deleted: a hit with no successful roll still must not fire.
3. **The `Slip` tile was drawn in Engagement's colour** while all 17 authored
   `evasion-window` cards sit on an avoid-faced Tempo lane. FIXED to `tempo`, with the
   limitation recorded in place: `WINDOW_LANE` is keyed by payoff kind, not by the firing
   card's lane, so it can only ever name one owner — `speed-window` has the same
   imprecision already, sitting on Tempo while the Axe's Kill-chain rows pay with it.
4. **`syncSummonerLine` reallocated `statusEffects` 10×/s** for every Splinter and Effigy
   summoner, which have no line to hold. FIXED with a presence guard.
5. **`evasion-window` grazes rather than dodges.** NOT FIXED — a design call, below.
6. **The plating-shred composition residual.** NOT a new finding: W1-E's fix *is* in place
   (`cardTotal / effect.stacks`), and what the review re-found independently is the
   residual W1-E itself declared — stacks added *later* multiply the per-stack value past
   the authored total. Representing "the max of two totals" when the sources disagree on
   stack count needs per-source contributions the record does not carry, which is a data
   model change beyond this run.
7. **Technique has one T1 shard family.** OVERSTATED as a reachability bug. Design §12 is
   explicit that there are **no tiered shard variants** and that lower-tier farming is a
   slow but legal route, so a T1-only source does not make the Broadsword's T2–T4 rows
   unbuyable. It is a pacing gap, below.

The review also verified as *not* problems: the `technique.power-pct` fold in

## Open for the designer

**Decisions, in the order they bite a playtest**

1. **The Plains boss walls the tutorial Striker, 0/11.** The first seal of the first biome,
   uncontended, at GM 30 — and it was a first-try win in September. Kit-shaped, since every
   other class beat it first try. This is the most playtest-blocking thing in the run.
2. **`evasion-window` grazes.** A window-only dodge has no gear `evadeMitigation` to stack
   on, so it falls back to `EVADE_MITIGATION_BASE` = 0.5: the avoid face's new payoff is a
   15% chance of a **half-hit that still applies the monster's ailments**, not a dodge. Two
   ways out, both design calls — make a window dodge a true dodge (stronger avoid, and it
   would make `evadeMitigation >= 1` reachable, which interacts with finding 2 above), or
   accept the graze and keep the help text honest. Nothing was changed, because either
   answer moves how strong avoid is.
3. **Gate levels collapse to 1 for a door into a biome that debuts at that tier** —
   Jungle/Desert at T2, Tundra/Volcanic at T3, Graveyard/Trench at T4 — because that biome
   has no content at the tier below, so there is nothing to have mastered. The boss capstone
   becomes the whole gate, which reads as §5's intent. A non-trivial level there needs a new
   rule, e.g. read that gate in the *band's* door biome rather than the new form's.
4. **The boss Lane A stopgap now pays four lanes that already had family sources.** §18.6's
   sentence is unqualified but `biomeDefensiveLaneAPackets` skipped weapon lineages, so the
   brief's "the Plains boss pays Technique shards" was false as written. Fixing it also
   gives Forest/Cave bosses 4 Tempo, Mountain 4 Empowered and Swamp 4 Affliction. Revertable
   to a bespoke "weapon Lane A with <2 sources" rule, but that is a second table in disguise.
5. **Shard geography after §22.** Tempo has **no** tier-1 Plains source at all now, and
   Technique's two T1 sources are **both in Plains** (Swarm plus the boss stopgap). A second
   biome fielding a Technique family would fix both halves.
6. **Coverage needed two named exemptions.** §22 moves exactly one lineage onto Technique,
   so it sits on 1 weapon and 0 non-weapon lineages against a ≥2 rule.
   `WEAPON_COVERAGE_BELOW_TWO` / `NON_WEAPON_COVERAGE_BELOW_TWO` are self-cleaning (both
   loops fail if a listed packet meets the rule). A second weapon carrier means taking a
   lane off a lineage already at the floor — after §22, Tempo/Affliction/Control/Engagement
   are each on exactly two.
7. **Bible open decision 2** (are Mountain/Cave/Swamp's T1 families the right ones?) is
   still not closed, and this run applied a monster pass on top of it.
8. **The T3 class path review** is still owed and still out of scope. `bulwark` and
   `barrier-tempo` have no tagged path at all, and both are weapon-ineligible, so no weapon
   keystone can name them either.

**Owed work that is not a decision**

- **The sixteen contract verdicts need re-measuring pooled across hosts** (~16 × 13 min).
  Until then §18 3b cannot arbitrate a `drops` move for any family.
- **Non-weapon reference profiles**, without which `bulwark` and `barrier-tempo` — two of
  the nine packets — cannot be measured on the punished side at all, and three families
  stay unmeasurable.
- **Mountain Crag T2 and Cave Brute T2** need their own N or cap.
- **A T2 member for `swamp-mire`, `jungle-stalker` and `desert-stalker`** — a pool fix, not
  a stat edit. Nine of 37 authored T2 blends do not resolve as named until then.
- **Canonical 1× tutorial evidence** for the five classes W2-B did not run, plus seals,
  Greatbear and gate at 1× for anyone. Now a realistic session rather than a ten-hour job.
- **A real-client pass on the farewell**, which no bot can reach.
- **The §22 ability pass** — roster audit, reauthored offers, bespoke per-ability keystones,
  T3 path re-homing — is a design session plus its own run.

**Provisional and flagged**

Everything run 3 flagged, plus: all 15 monster rows and the banner rewrite; the Consort
line's 0.15/0.15, taken raw from `PAYOFF_UNIT` and unbenched, where a standing condition
needs the bench more than a window does; `evasion-window`'s unit (0.15 / 2500 ms, sized
against its nearest siblings, no bench history); the whole Technique packet —
`PACKET_BUDGET.technique = 1` enters at parity, unmeasured, with `technique-refund` 0.35
and `technique-window` 0.30/4000 ms, `TRIGGER_FIRES_PER_PULL` 2 from the loadout the bench
actually measures, `STAT_UNIT.techniquePowerPct` 0.1, the lane colour `#ff7fd4`, and
`PACKET_FACE.technique = 'avoid'` which is provisional **and currently unread**, since no
non-weapon lineage carries the lane.

**Eight repo blind spots W1-G's typecheck could not catch**, all written up in its progress
note so a tenth packet does not rediscover them: `PAYOFF_UNIT` is an array not an exhaustive
`Record` (a missing unit throws at *runtime*, and only if a card uses it); `PAYOFF_BLURB`
falls back to programmer text shipped to the player; `ABILITY_NODE_FX` is keyed by a
`"lane/payoff"` string; `OFFENSIVE_NODE_PAYOFFS`/`DEFENSIVE_NODE_PAYOFFS` membership is a
test not a type; `WINDOW_LANE` and `LINEAGE_BUFFS` are partial; `PAYOFF_IS_TIMED` is a
`Set<string>` where a typo is not a type error; `combatFx.ts`'s switch has a `default`, so a
new fx key compiles and draws nothing; and one buff tile means three hand-maintained lists.

**A near-miss worth knowing about.** W2-A ran a `taskkill` filtered on `node.exe` while
trying to stop its own bench. It matched nothing — the filter it used excludes windowless
processes — and it verified afterwards that other sessions' processes were alive. Nothing
was lost and it did not repeat it, but with several sessions sharing this machine it could
have taken out unrelated work.

### Findings against `develop`, from the misdirected first review

Not this run's diff; recorded so they are not lost. They are against the Phase 0/0b
Recovery work on `develop` (`3204ce52`).

1. `server/bench/phase0Recovery.ts:193` — `oldPctFor` is called before the
   `if (model === 'old')` guard, so the default `PHASE0_MODEL=new` + `PHASE0_ARM=charm` run
   dies with `AssertionError: no pre-0b value for plains-charm-t1`. **The bench's primary
   arm is currently unrunnable.**
2. `server/src/systems/player/progression/questSystem.ts:22` — tier-up bumps `playerTier`
   with no stat recalc, so base Recovery stays at the previous tier's value (6.5 instead of
   10 on T1→T2) until an unrelated recalc fires.
3. `client/src/hud/stat/StatPanel.tsx:436` — `${player.recovery} HP/s` is unrounded;
   realistic charm sums render as e.g. `18.799999999999997 HP/s`.
4. `server/src/systems/classes/archetypes/summoner/spawn.ts:40` — minion out-of-combat
   regen is derived from the owner's **in-combat** Recovery, so it is ~5%/s naked at T1
   against the player's fixed 10%/s, and now scales with Recovery gear. The "same clock"
   comment is false.
5. `shared/src/systems/stats.ts:440` — `core.recovery-mult` still `Math.round`s Recovery,
   quantizing the now-fractional HP/s rate (losing ~17% of the Jungle core's bonus at T1).
