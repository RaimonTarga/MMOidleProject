## Build reasoning and alternatives

The catalogue records a source-based reason for every package. Farming starts with
usable AoE, with Slam for selected high-Attack packages and Sweep for fast delivery.
All Mountain packages retain named reactive Brace; T3+ boss packages use persistent
Defensive stance to complement the known cooldown gap. A custom Brace rule suppresses
its default low-HP trigger. No claim of complete impact coverage follows from legality.

Conduit retains Orbit except the historical T3 balanced/mid farm policy and melee
Champion. Blunderbuss and Champion declare close-range exceptions. Channel packages
omit Orbit while retaining telegraph escape. On-hit/channel paths use real on-hit
equipment; fixed-cadence/high-Attack paths and empowered paths receive explicit
weapon choices. Survivalist, Accelerant, Arcanist, Bruiser, Duelist and Catalyst are
used where their production delivery and eligibility support them. Relics vary by
path; fixed Conduit formations keep potency/HP-payment tradeoffs visible.

Unselected alternatives, for a later owner decision only: Offensive vs Defensive
boss stance; Surge fast/medium weapon for earlier Overdrive vs the selected heavy
Attack weapon; Apprentice light high-Attack axe vs rapid stack application; Squire
Arcanist/Slam vs weapon-driven Sweep; Conduit Survivalist vs Catalyst, Orbit vs no
Orbit, and signed-frequency relics vs Equilibrium. None is a hidden arm or extra run.

## Reporting contract

`results.json` is one compact row per scheduled case with source/treatment/seed/fixture
and relative detail links. Keep `ready.json`, authoritative terminal proof, summary,
events, samples and `conduit.json` separately. Do not inline histories/readbacks in
the overview. Publish failed/unrun cases as null with availability reasons, not zeros.

For ordinary rows require actual damaged targets; episode membership is not exposure.
Zero damage, aborted casts, missing secondary targets and short exposure are diagnostic
flags, not automatic blanket invalidation. Report completed kills, partial/censored
targets, terminal HP/barrier, duration and cause together. Death-truncated kills/min
is not sustainable farming throughput; capped duration is not a kill time.

Conduit records initialize/spawn/death/removal/sacrifice, role identity, lifetime,
first actual attack, queue changes plus 1Hz snapshots, time-weighted body/offense/proc
availability including Champion owner share, ready-HP-blocked tick duration, attempts,
payments, attributable queue healing, owner exposure, and censored collapse/recovery.
Damage delivery is observed synchronous primary-target HP decrease by intact/depleted
state and target HP phase, with target/exposure denominators. It is not predicted DPS.
Delayed/secondary damage that lacks unique attribution is null with a reason. Unknown
removal causes remain unknown. Boss wake events are at -100ms, outside measured time.

Do not compare rates without adequate comparable target/phase exposure. No-loss rows
are not evidence of reconstruction improvement. Distinguish timer delay, HP blocking,
replacement churn, engagement failures and intact-formation delivery. More useful
surviving formation and outcomes can justify adoption review; excessive churn/explosion
payoffs or redirection warrant rejection/revision, never compensation on another axis.

Publish results under a new `reports/player-fast-pass/breadth-run-01-review/`, including
sealed manifest/identity, readable decision summary, per-case receipts and terminal
proof, relevant diagnostic excerpts, and hash/path inventory of raw artifacts. Preserve
original bytes and historical reports. Commit/push scoped results through the owner's
normal workflow; report full publication SHA and entrypoint separately from measured
source. End with at most five findings and a short alternative ledger. Do not generate
or launch a follow-on packet. Adoption and next direction return to the command center.
