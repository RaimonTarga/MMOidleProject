## Measurement rules and evidence boundary

For each target species, clean TTK is computed per seed from killed targets with no observed HP regain and no unfinished/censored status. All TTK values in the tables are seconds. The cell species value is the median of the three eligible seed medians. The bracket after each species is K/U/R/M: killed records / unfinished or censored records / records with observed HP regain / missing seed medians. Regain overlaps the other categories. A dash is a missing seed median, not a zero-second kill.

Cell K/U/R/M uses the same all-target accounting and sums the missing species-seed medians. The adjacent clean/records value counts clean target records over all target records. The mixed-body value is the reporter aggregate clean body metric: it is not an elite median and not a pack-clear duration. Single-hit kills can legitimately show 0.00 seconds. Player/minion beats are cooldown/attack timestamp changes; they are not guaranteed hits.

There are 19 missing species-seed medians across the matrix. A species with M=2 has fewer than two eligible seeds and is inconclusive under the packet; M=1 is retained only as a limited two-seed descriptive value. No class or biome disposition selects favorable surviving seeds.

Incoming source totals in the cell table are summed over that cell’s three runs and show the three largest player-targeted source names, followed by direct/DoT/debt HP totals. Conduit rows can have zero player-targeted damage while minions receive pressure; their player/minion attack beats are kept separately. The full event stream remains the source of truth.

Exposure flags below come from a read-only reconstruction of the packet’s samples.jsonl rule: maximum internal quiet or terminal quiet at least 30 seconds. This is descriptive because the official exposure script was not reached. Quiet survival is not safety. All 504 samples streams had zero blocked-approach samples in this reconstruction.
