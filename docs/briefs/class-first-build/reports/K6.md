## Not done, or unsure

- **Colossus's twist is an example.** "share one colossal twist (e.g. their hits shake the ground around them), not a third trait per summon". The card says they share one extra trick. It does not lock the ground shake. The starting numbers "+100% HP and +50% damage over the base summon" are not in `params`.
- **Elite's "area damage" line was read as a budget note.** "The extras are Elite's area damage." Not written as "elites deal area damage". Starting numbers "+50% HP, +25% damage" are not in `params`.
- **The Desert Soul's stack has no payoff.** "Each different summon type that hits the marked target adds a stack." The card says each different kind of summon adds to the mark, and does not say what the mark then does.
- **Devour does not say from whom.** "a summon that has lived a while starts stealing back HP." The card keeps "stealing health back" and does not choose the enemy or health the summon already lost.
- **Legion's steps are undesigned.** "at visible thresholds (at 4 copies they do X, at 8 Y)". §7.15 item 4 lists Legion's thresholds as playtest. The card says "in clear steps" and does not state 4 or 8.
- **Playtest numbers stayed out of `params`.** Swarm's half-or-full strength (§7.15 item 1), the Pack Strike's 8 seconds, and the second Soul's Command cost (§7.15 item 2).
- **Swarm's "No temporaries in the baseline."** Left off the card. It reads as an authoring note, not an effect the player sees.
- **Cinderling's ramp.** The T3 table says "hotter (stronger) as the fight's Heat rises". The paragraph above it says the ramp is the summon's own, "not the Volcanic's Heat", and that it "grows hotter the longer it fights, in every biome." The card follows that paragraph.
- **Digits taken off two decided lines.** The Executioner's shots "hit twice as hard", and Final Command lands "at double strength". Both cards say "much harder".
- **Last Stand's "at 1 HP"** is "a scrap of health" on the card.
- **Barkward's "stun or control"** is written "stun, root or knockdown", the player words for control. Say if control meant only the stun.
- **Cave Brute's cell is only "Heavy tank, 2 Command".** The cost is the `command` field. The card says it soaks the hits that come at you, which is the tank role in §7.3, not a trick the cell states.
