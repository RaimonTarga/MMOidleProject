# T1 boss pass — brief (2026-10-02)

Single Opus session, serial, in `../mmo-lineages` on a new branch `balance/t1-boss-pass`
off `feat/lineage-data-model` (tip `8bf2fadb`). Merge back into `feat/lineage-data-model`
at the end. No other session uses this worktree during the run. Never `pnpm install`,
never push, strip CR only on files you touch, never kill processes by image name.

## Why

The canonical 1x six-class tutorial sweep (docs/guided-tutorial-plan.md, 2026-10-02) found
three class-shaped walls that predate the progression rework and were masked by 25x
evidence: Gnarled Greatbear vs Squire (0/13, lost at 11–16% left) and Apprentice (0/11,
5–18% left); Crag Behemoth vs Striker (0/12, 7–28% left; one burst sequence does 164 into a
228 pool from ~60% HP). The Razorback is fixed for all six classes by the weapon fold
`74ab24ac`. Read docs/briefs/run4-report-2026-10-02.md from "The bisect" to the end, and
design_docs/progression-rework-design.md §23, before touching anything. The bisect proved
the monster pass, the pool raise and the recovery rework are NOT the cause; do not reopen
them.

## Designer decisions (locked)

1. **Scope: all five T1 bosses** (`shared/src/data/monsters/bossesT1.ts`): Tusked
   Razorback, Gnarled Greatbear, Crag Behemoth, Grave Toadeater, Obsidian Broodmother.
   Boss rows only. Mob rows, Recovery, `PLAYER_POOL_FACTOR_BY_TIER`, T2+ bosses are out.
2. **Bar:** every class beats every T1 boss at GM 30 on the tutorial's own boss kit;
   the hardest class/boss pairing wins **≥ 70%** of attempts; **no class wins 100% on all
   five** (each class must still lose to at least one boss sometimes). Report the
   win-rate matrix; both halves of the bar must hold.
3. **Behemoth:** trim the one burst sequence (the dominant hit's magnitude or multiplier)
   so it is survivable from ~60% HP for the Striker. Do not touch its HP first. It stays
   the tier's burst exam.
4. **Greatbear:** whole-fight pressure (HP and/or attack), sized to the measured margins
   (losses at 5–18% left → start around −15%, then measure).
5. **Authorship:** you propose and APPLY, provisionally, one commit per boss, with a
   before/after table in the report. The designer reverts rows they dislike. Prefer the
   smallest number of levers per boss; never move a number you did not measure.
6. **Second seal:** currently Mountain for every class (`TUTORIAL_SEALS` in
   `shared/src/tutorial/scripts/plans.ts`; the 2026-09-29 Forest→Mountain swap rested on
   25x evidence). After tuning, decide from the lab: if Mountain is still the harder seal
   for most classes, move the seal back to Forest for every class and re-run the sweep.
   Record the decision and the numbers behind it either way.
7. **Recovery, report only:** per class and boss, the HP trajectory, share of damage
   healed back in-fight, time spent under 25% HP. The designer's hypothesis is that the
   flat-recovery change is why classes now lose to bosses; the bisect says otherwise.
   Give the numbers that let them judge it in the playtest. Change nothing.

## Order

1. Baseline matrix: six classes × five bosses in the lab, current numbers, ≥ 10 seeds.
   Commit the plan file and results under `reports/t1-boss-pass/`.
2. Behemoth burst trim (decision 3) → re-matrix the Behemoth column.
3. Greatbear (decision 4) → re-matrix its column.
4. Any other pairing below the 70% floor from step 1 → smallest lever, re-matrix.
5. Check the ceiling half of the bar; if a class is 100% on all five, do NOT buff a boss
   to fix it — report it and let the designer decide.
6. Seal decision (6).
7. Canonical 1x sweep on the committed tip. Tests: `pnpm test -- boss tutorial`,
   `weaponCurve`, any test that pins a boss number; `pnpm typecheck`. Related tests only.
8. Report: `docs/briefs/t1-boss-pass-report-2026-10-02.md` — before/after table per
   boss with the lever and the reason, the two win-rate matrices, the seal decision, the
   Recovery section (7), the sweep ledger appended to docs/guided-tutorial-plan.md, and
   an explicit list of every number moved so the designer can revert by row. State what
   you could not verify.
9. Merge `balance/t1-boss-pass` into `feat/lineage-data-model` (no push). Leave the
   scratch DBs; the designer drops them.
