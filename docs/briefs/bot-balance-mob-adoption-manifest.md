## Defence coupling — exact, not rounded

Every coupled field preserves an **absolute** budget by scaling the percentage inversely.
Author the exact expression or carry full precision:

| Species | Absolute budget | Authored percentage |
|---|---|---|
| `granite-mammoth` | ward `0.25 × 1150 = 287.5` | `0.25 * 1150 / 13800` = 1/48 = 0.0208333… — the rounded `0.0208` gives 287.04, short by 0.46 |
| `glacial-direbear` | shield `0.22 × 1221 = 268.62` | `0.22 * 1221 / 4884` = 0.055 exactly |

The Durability22 installer also rescales every `shield` action nested inside `monsterAbilities`
by `before/after`. A patch must carry those too — a dash in a summary row must not hide a real
coupling. `granite-titan`'s Granite Barrier is **not** rescaled: its HP does not move, only its
attack, so `wardPct` stays 0.25.

**Resolved 2026-09-18 — the complete coupling set, and one deliberate divergence.** Three nested
`monsterAbilities` shield actions exist among these species, and all three are now held at their
pre-adoption absolute budget:

| Species | Ability | Absolute budget | Authored `shieldPct` | Tested by its own installer? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `elder-leviathan` | Carapace Renewal | `0.18 × 5880 = 1058.4` | `0.06` exactly | **Yes** — D22 scales it |
| `magma-brute` | Molten Guard | `0.14 × 2000 = 280` | `0.14 * 2000 / 3000` | **No** — D15 never scaled it |
| `magma-salamander` | Obsidian Shell | `0.28 × 2904 = 813.12` | `0.14` exactly | **No** — D23 never scaled it |

The Durability22 installer's own header states the rule — *"Preserve absolute ward/shell/self-shatter
budgets"* — and the adoption applies it **uniformly**. The consequence is recorded, not hidden:
`magma-brute` and `magma-salamander` are now **weaker** than in the runs that selected their HP,
where their shells scaled with the pool (420 and 1626.24). Durability37 Block I families 2 and 3
exist partly to observe that. Do not "restore" the scaled shells without a decision.

**One field deliberately NOT rescaled.** `cragback-rhino`'s `enemySoftCap.capPct` is also taken of
`maxHp`, but it is a *self-relative* statement — a cap at 25% of the monster's own pool — and the
retained installer never touched it. Leaving it reproduces the configuration that was actually
measured. Its absolute clip threshold therefore moves `275 → 1650`, so the soft cap clips far less
often. That is a real behaviour change and is recorded here rather than discovered later.

`glacial-direbear` couples **two** fields, not one: the barrier `0.22 → 0.055` and the
self-shatter `selfDamagePct 0.14 → 0.035`, holding 268.62 and 170.94 respectively.

## Jungle, added 2026-09-18 after Durability34

Durability34 tested the HP-only package across 72 observations, six roots, two nodes, both arms
full-window with zero deaths. Measured clean body TTK moved as intended and the fast bodies did
not:

| Species | Clean TTK control → candidate | Fast bodies |
|---|---|---|
| `emerald-constrictor` | 3.27 s → 6.50 s | `thornback-lizard` 1.90 → 1.90 s |
| `apex-silverback` | 2.85 s → 5.95 s | `hunting-panther` 1.85 → 1.80 s |

Apex ramp exposure rose with the longer body (ramped share 81.9% → 91.7%, p90 gross 124 → 137),
measured from existing damage evidence rather than asserted.

**Explicitly unverified: the cross-tier ladder.** There is no measured T3 TTK anywhere in this
campaign — the "2.90 s T3 anchor" in the D34 report was the T4 apex baseline, and the real T3
citation was authored HP 2090. Authored HP ordering is **not** the acceptance test for a
duration ladder. Before Jungle pacing is called complete, compare compatible existing T2/T3
role-timing evidence against T4 and, where it is absent, fold one limited check into the
consolidated regression. If the actual duration ladder is still reversed, make one deliberate
revision rather than re-deriving from authored HP.

Four things this table is not:

- **Not measured successful values.** They are a command-center proposal derived from D36.
  Projection = reported centre × adopted HP ÷ D36 HP, which assumes roughly unchanged effective
  output. Real pressure, recovery, mechanics and target selection can invalidate it.
  **Durability37 Block J is what checks them.**
- **Not a factor on 2900/3400.** The T4 elites' *source* values were 1450 and 1700; D36 ran them
  under an overlay. The adopted numbers are absolute finals.
- **Not a universal elite-duration floor**, and not the Trench 40–60 s goal applied to Jungle.
  Cave and Mountain guides are unchanged.
- **Not T2.** T2 Jungle is deliberately untouched: it already sits at the intended ~11 s and is
  the ladder's anchor.

Working intent: roughly **11 → 15 → 23 s** for the primary lineage and **4 → 5 → 6 s** for the
fast lineages. `emerald-constrictor` stays the separate, tougher T4 control predator. Attack,
defense, ramp/finisher parameters, cadence, movement, ecology, density, player loadouts and
rewards are all unchanged — more HP simply exposes the existing ramp and crowd pressure longer,
so survival and recovery need testing rather than inheriting D36's zero deaths.
