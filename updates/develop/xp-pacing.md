## Mastery pacing

- Biome mastery now takes longer at higher tiers, so each tier lasts long enough to matter.
  Aim: about 5 minutes per biome at T1, 15 at T2, 30 at T3 and 60 at T4.
- T2 mastery is faster than before (3,750 XP per tier, down from 5,000).
- T3 and T4 mastery need much more XP: 42,000 and 600,000 per tier, up from 7,000 and 9,000.
- T4 Tundra and Desert give more mastery XP per kill (×1.8 and ×2.4), so they keep pace
  with other T4 biomes. Essence and catalyst drops are unchanged.
- Existing playtest characters are not converted to the new curve. Start a fresh character
  for accurate pacing.
