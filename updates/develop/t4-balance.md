## Tier 4 class balance

- **Voidwalker:** the stored-energy discharge is now reduced by enemy armor and damage reduction, like any other hit. It used to ignore them. The early execute uses the same damage to decide when to trigger.
- **Berserker:** each Rampage stack speeds up attacks by 30 ms instead of 60 ms.
- **Juggernaut:** Crescendo still climbs for as long as the fight lasts, but past +100% finisher damage it grows much more slowly.
