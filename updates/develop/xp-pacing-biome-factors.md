## Mastery pacing, every biome

- T2 mastery is slower in most biomes, landing near 15 minutes. Cave, Swamp, Plains, Mountain,
  Forest and Jungle give less mastery XP per kill (×0.35–0.75). Desert is unchanged.
- T3 biomes are evened out to about 30 minutes. Swamp, Cave and Mountain give less mastery XP
  (×0.6–0.9); Tundra, Desert and Jungle give more (×1.2–1.4).
- T4 Mountain, Jungle, Graveyard and Trench give more mastery XP per kill (×2.6–3.4), so they
  keep pace with Tundra and Desert.
- Essence and catalyst drops are unchanged.
- New auto-combat condition for summoners: **Formation Broken** (half or fewer of your summons
  standing). Pair it with Flee to back off and rebuild.
