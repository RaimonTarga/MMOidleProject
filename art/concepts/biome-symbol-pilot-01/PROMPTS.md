# Biome Symbol Pilot 01

Generated on 2026-07-24 with Codex's built-in image generation.

## Shared direction

- Use case: stylized-concept.
- A single original game UI biome symbol, centered and isolated.
- Designed to remain legible at 25–34 px.
- Low-detail, emblematic terrain language inspired by the readability of collectible-card resource symbols, without copying any existing symbol or trademarked shape.
- No enclosing circle, badge, frame, text, letters, watermark, gradients, lighting, or surface texture.
- Thick near-black outline, strong silhouette, large internal shapes, and no more than three flat colors.
- Square canvas with generous clear space around the symbol.

The generated chroma-key backgrounds were removed locally with a soft matte and despill. Each transparent result was then normalized to a 256 × 256 master with consistent padding. The comparison sheet shows the masters at 64, 34, and 25 px over representative map-tile colors.

## Visual variants

### A — Void-cut sigil

A bold filled silhouette whose biome identity is reinforced by one memorable negative-space cut. Shapes can be slightly organic, but must remain compact and readable.

### B — Carved elemental rune

A flat, geometric, slightly asymmetrical symbol made from a few stonecut shapes. Prioritize structural clarity and consistent visual weight.

## Forest

- **Void-cut:** A compact branching tree with canopy, trunk, and leaf masses merged into one emblem; a winding path is cut through the lower center. Emerald, moss green, and near-black. Magenta chroma key.
- **Rune:** An angular evergreen crown made from three interlocking triangular branches with a sharp central path. Pine green, lichen green, and near-black. Magenta chroma key.

## Cave

- **Void-cut:** A heavy cave-mouth arch with two blunt stalactite teeth and a deep central entrance. Charcoal, slate violet, and pale stone. Green chroma key.
- **Rune:** An angular inverted arch with two blocky pillars, a faceted roof, a black triangular entrance, and a stalactite notch. Charcoal, slate, and pale limestone. Green chroma key.

## Volcanic

- **Void-cut:** A squat broken volcano with a central flame eruption and one glowing fissure. Ember orange, crimson, and near-black. Green chroma key.
- **Rune:** A broad broken-chevron cone with a central zigzag fissure and three shard-like flames. Basalt, burnt red, and molten orange. Green chroma key.

## Trench

- **Void-cut:** Two descending wave walls enclosing a deep chasm, with a small pressure-ring motif. Navy, teal, and near-black. Magenta chroma key.
- **Rune:** Mirrored wave chevrons descending to a central point with a pressure ring above. Navy, ocean blue, and pale teal. Magenta chroma key.
