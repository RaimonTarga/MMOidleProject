# Biome Symbol Set 01

Generated on 2026-07-24 with Codex's built-in image generation. The existing
Forest, Cave, Volcanic, and Trench masters from `biome-symbol-pilot-01` were
supplied as style references only.

## Shared specification

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset type: game UI biome icon, readable at 25–34 px
- One centered, isolated carved-elemental-rune symbol
- Flat geometric, slightly asymmetrical stonecut shapes
- Very low detail, bold silhouette, large internal regions, and a thick
  near-black outline matching the pilot icons
- No enclosing circle, badge, shield, frame, background scene, text, letters,
  watermark, gradients, lighting, texture, or trademarked card-game shapes
- No more than three subject colors
- Perfectly flat chroma-key background, removed locally with a soft matte and
  despill

## Icon subjects

- **Clearing:** A shallow angular grassy hollow, two low grass tufts opening
  away from the center, and three short daylight rays. Spring green and pale
  warm gold.
- **Sanctuary:** Two inward-leaning refuge pillars sheltering a suspended
  diamond light above an open doorway. Deep teal and pale mint.
- **Mountain:** One dominant summit, two offset ridges, and a pale zigzag snow
  face. Charcoal slate, cool gray, and pale stone.
- **Plains:** Two shallow horizon bands with three wind-swept grass blades.
  Muted olive and pale wheat gold.
- **Swamp:** Two bent reeds around a winding bog pool and one rising mire-spore
  diamond. Murky olive and sickly yellow-green.
- **Jungle:** Three overlapping spear-shaped tropical leaves crossed by one
  short angular vine. Deep emerald and bright jade.
- **Tundra:** A broad six-pronged ice crystal rising from a cracked ice shelf.
  Cold navy, icy cyan, and pale frost.
- **Desert:** Two interlocking angular dunes beneath a diamond sun, with one
  wind-cut notch. Deep ochre and warm sand gold.
- **Graveyard / Wasteland:** A cracked leaning grave marker or standing stone
  emerging from split barren ground between dead thorn branches. Bruised
  violet-gray and pale bone.
- **The Abyss:** Mirrored jagged void pillars descending around a narrow central
  rift, with a broken lavender diamond above it. Deep violet and pale lavender.

Green subjects used a magenta chroma key; the remaining subjects used a green
chroma key. Transparent results were alpha-trimmed, proportionally normalized
to 256 × 256 with consistent padding, then reduced to the established 35/36 ×
32 atlas frames.
