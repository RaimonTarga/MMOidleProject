# UI redesign Phase 10C final symbols

Generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-07-24. The approved
`art/style/ui-navigation.png` image was supplied as the style reference. Each
candidate used a flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background, was processed with the
image-generation skill's `remove_chroma_key.py` helper, and was reduced to
32x32 with nearest-neighbor sampling.

## Shared direction

Bold, flat old-school fantasy UI pixel art with crisp stepped clusters, a
compact silhouette, and the approved charcoal-iron, muted-bronze, and pale-cyan
palette. One centered isolated symbol, readable at 18x18, with no text,
surrounding control frame, scene, shadow, or particles.

## Craft Upgrade

A compact front-facing forged iron item plate with one broad pale-cyan upward
chevron rising through its center and two small muted-bronze reinforcement
corners. It must communicate improving equipment without resembling the Forge
anvil.

## Rune Situation

A broad angular charcoal keystone shard containing one pale-cyan open
eye-shaped rune, with exactly two small muted-bronze timing notches on its upper
edge. The eye is geometric and abstract rather than anatomical.

## Rune Response

A broad angular charcoal keystone shard split open by one bold pale-cyan
right-pointing energy arrow, with exactly two small muted-bronze impact notches
on the right edge. It pairs with the Situation rune while remaining visibly
distinct.
