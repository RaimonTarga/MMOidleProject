## Dungeon skull

Create one extremely simple front-facing skull rune for a fantasy world-map
dungeon marker, ultimately displayed at 14–16 pixels. Use one broad ivory
cranium with a thick near-black carved edge, exactly two large triangular eye
voids, and two or three blocky lower teeth. Keep it centered, symmetrical,
low-detail, and readable as a strong terrain-style symbol. No jaw detail,
bones, flames, crown, text, badge, enclosing circle, or frame.

Render on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background with generous
padding and no shadow, gradient, texture, floor, reflection, or key color in
the subject.

The production file was chroma-keyed to transparency, normalized on a 256×256
master, and reduced to a 16×16 atlas source.
