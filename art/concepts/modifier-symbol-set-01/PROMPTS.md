# Modifier symbol set 01

Generated with the built-in image workflow on 2026-07-24. Each source used the
existing carved-rune biome symbols as style references.

## Shared art direction

Create one tiny, low-detail fantasy map modifier symbol: a bold carved rune
silhouette, Magic-style terrain-symbol readability, ivory stone face with a
near-black carved edge, chunky stepped pixel-art geometry, centered, no text,
no letters, no badge, no enclosing circle, no scene, and no cast shadow. It
must remain distinct at 13–16 pixels. Render on a perfectly flat chroma-green
background for clean transparency extraction.

## Subjects

- **Alacrity:** three separate swept chevrons or wind-feathers surging upward
  and right; light, rapid, and aerodynamic.
- **Brutality:** one heavy downward impact wedge split by a blunt central
  crack; massive, forceful, and slow.
- **Blight:** one corrupted droplet or seed with a hooked thorn and a small
  inner decay void; toxic, organic, and withered.
- **Volatility:** one fractured four-point crystal or unstable star with an
  offset split core; explosive, unstable, and sharp.
- **Predation:** three converging fang or claw points forming a compact hunting
  mark; focused, stalking, and lethal.

Production files were chroma-keyed to transparency, normalized on 256×256
masters, and nearest-neighbor reduced to 16×16 atlas sources.
