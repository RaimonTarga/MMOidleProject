# Catalyst symbol set 01

Generated with the built-in image workflow on 2026-07-24. Each prompt used
the corresponding accepted modifier master plus the original neutral catalyst
placeholder as references.

Shared construction:

- one broad upright faceted shard with an extremely thick near-black border;
- the exact family modifier rune as a centered ivory-and-black inlay;
- at most two or three large facets, with no glints, particles, text, or frame;
- flat `#00ff00` chroma background for local removal;
- production at 32x32, explicitly checked at both 32 and 16 pixels.

Family colors:

- Alacrity: cyan `#4fc3f7`
- Brutality: muted red `#e57373`
- Blight: leaf green `#81c784`
- Volatility: purple `#ba68c8`
- Predation: amber `#ffb74d`

`set-comparison.png` records the accepted literal-size comparison. The Blight
matte received an edge-only cleanup so its green crystal body remained intact.
