# Modifier pictogram candidates 01

Preview-only exploration generated with the built-in image workflow on
2026-07-24. Nothing in this directory is wired into the atlas.

## Style anchor

Every candidate used the approved
`modifier-symbol-set-01/alacrity-master.png` wing as its reference:

- expressive ivory pictogram rather than a uniform carved rune;
- restrained near-black edge;
- angular, hand-cut geometry;
- very few large interior regions;
- explicit readability at 16 pixels.

The generated subjects were:

- Brutality A: chipped executioner axe.
- Brutality B: clenched armored gauntlet.
- Blight A: bitten, hooked withered leaf.
- Blight B: drooping mushroom with two decay holes.
- Volatility A: classic two-bend lightning bolt. The first forked version was
  rejected during the literal-size check and regenerated without the fork.
- Volatility B: splitting energy core with three burst points.
- Predation A: three-clawed predator paw.
- Predation B: twin descending fangs.

Sources were generated on flat `#00ff00`, chroma-keyed to transparency,
normalized to 256x256, and deliberately reduced to a two-tone 16x16 pixel
preview. `map-context-candidates.png` shows the exact 16px icons inside the
actual 18px family-colored badge and a 70px map-node mockup.
