# UI prototypes — the five scenes

The checklist every prototype builds against, so the five show the same moments.
Brief: [`docs/briefs/ui-prototypes-run-2026-10-04.md`](../../docs/briefs/ui-prototypes-run-2026-10-04.md).
Data: [`data.json`](data.json), generated by `server/scripts/_uiProtoExport.ts` from the
client's own models (`weaponPageModel.ts`, `rhythmReadoutModel.ts`, `targetStatusMeta.ts`,
`statusHelp.ts`, `areaInformationModel.ts`) at the commit in `data.commit`.

## Loading the data

- `<script src="../data.js"></script>` sets `window.UI_PROTO_DATA` (the same object as
  `data.json`). Use it rather than `fetch('../data.json')`, which fails when the page is
  opened from disk.
- Every name, sentence, cost, requirement, colour and number on a card, a badge, a banner
  or a toast comes from this object. Never retype one. If you need a number the export
  lacks, show a visible `[n]` and list it in NOTES.md.
- Art paths in the data are repo-relative (`art/src/...`, `client/public/...`). From
  `reports/ui-prototypes/<x>/index.html`, prefix them with `../../../`. A `null` path means
  there is no art; draw a plain shape and say so.
- No card has a `description` in the export. That is deliberate: no flavour text, one
  sentence per card (design §24.6).

## The frame every scene shares

- **Who:** a **Slinger** (reload root, no frame chosen yet), the class the tutorial
  teaches the Dagger to (`tutorialPicks`, Slinger row).
- **Worn:** `daggerScenes.gear`: Poison Dagger, Shaded Bindings, Murk Eye, Fleet Boots.
  Only the Dagger has a page; the other three are plain items (design §24.5).
- **Player numbers:** `daggerScenes.playerStats`: max HP **193**, Attack **26**,
  **1.98** swings/s, move speed **157**. Current HP **171 / 193** in every combat scene.
- **Levels and wallet** (scene 2's state, `daggerScenes.pages[key=scene2].wallet`):
  Swamp 4, Plains 4, Forest 4, Mountain 3, Global Mastery 15; **95 Rot** essence,
  **9 Venom**, **4 Shackle**, **2 Bone** Shards; **0 of 3** Swamp capstones.
- **Owned cards:** Coated Edge (the Affliction lean), active. Into the Wound is bought
  during scene 2, and is owned from scene 3 on.
- **Place:** Blackwater Crown (`node-t1-swamp-04`), Swamp, Mire territory, pays **Venom
  Shards**.
- **The game is dark.** Match the client's palette: `client/src/ui/lineage/weaponPage.css`
  and the mockup's tokens (`--bg #14120f`, `--panel #1d1a16`, `--ink #ece4d4`,
  `--gold #d9b25c`); lane colours come from `packets[lane].color`. Fonts: the mockup uses
  Alegreya SC and IBM Plex Sans (Google Fonts); the floating word uses Cinzel.

## Every prototype has

- One self-contained `index.html` in its own folder, inline CSS and JS, plus `../data.js`.
  No external scripts beyond the CDN allowlist (cdnjs, jsdelivr, unpkg); none needed.
- It **opens on scene 1**, with a **scene switcher** (1–5), a **desktop / portrait
  switcher**, and a **first-time toggle**.
- **Desktop** is a 1280 × 720 frame, **portrait** a 390 × 844 frame, drawn at that size
  and scaled down to fit the window (CSS transform), never reflowed by the window. A
  portrait frame is a phone: thumb-reachable controls, no hover-only information.
- **The first-time toggle** overlays what the eye should land on **first, second, third**:
  three numbered markers on three elements, everything else dimmed. Nothing else.
- **A URL hash the comparison page can drive**, read on load and on `hashchange`:
  `#scene=1..5&view=desktop|portrait&first=0|1&embed=0|1`.
  `embed=1` hides your own switchers and any explanation panel, and **plays the scene's
  interaction by itself on a loop** (scene 3 alternates the pure and composed pairs), so
  a live iframe shows the scene working without a click. Without `embed`, the scene waits
  for the viewer's clicks.
- Real interaction where the scene has it: open the page, buy a card, land hits, fire the
  verb, arrive at a node.
- A `NOTES.md` beside it (see the end of this file).

## The three structural answers (the same in every prototype)

1. **The trees are reached from a Gear tab** that lists the worn items, the weapon first
   (mockup rule 1, the designer's call). Clicking the equipped weapon may also open the
   page, but the tab is the front door.
2. **The target shows one badge per damage-over-time record, with an icon.** Prototype the
   state mockup rule 3 assumes: **one record per weapon**. On the Dagger with the
   Affliction pair that is ONE Poison badge whose number is the damage still owed; the
   lean's light bleed and the verb's re-dose feed that one record. This is the designer's
   undecided **one-DoT rule**: your NOTES.md must say you assume it. The card sentences
   stay the export's (they still describe today's separate bleed); do not rewrite them.
   For reference, today the same target carries three records, two of which render as
   `Lineage.Lean Bleed` and `Lineage.Hemorrhage` with no icon and no help
   (`statuses.target`, `metaIsFallback: true`). Use the existing icons in
   `statuses.target[].icon` and `statuses.playerDebuffIcons`.
3. **The node banner names the family and the shard** (mockup rule 4, design §24.3), e.g.
   the export's `arrival.discovery`: "Mire territory · every kill here drops Venom Shards".

## The prototype combat rule (illustrative, the same in all five)

The scenes need numbers that move, and the five must move alike. This is a UI rule built
from the export's numbers, **not the server's DoT formula**; say so if a viewer could
mistake it for balance.

- The Slinger swings every **500 ms** (1.98/s). Every swing hits (ignore the Ooze's
  evasion and the Slinger's reload pause) for **26** (`playerStats.attack`).
- **Poison owed** on the target: each hit adds `26 × weaponDot.convPct` = **13** (the
  Dagger's reservoir, `weapons[lineageId=dagger].t1Form.weaponDot`), plus, while Coated
  Edge is the active lean, `26 × 0.10` = **2.6** (its light bleed). Under the one-record
  assumption both go into the one Poison record. Every **1 s** the record ticks for a
  quarter of what it owes (round up); the badge shows what is still owed, rounded.
- **Into the Wound, pure pair** (Coated Edge + Into the Wound): a hit on a target that
  owes poison fires it if its clock is clear; it **adds what the target still owes** (the
  re-dose, `payoff.kind = redose`, magnitude 1); then its clock runs **5 s**
  (`trigger.capMs`). The ring is a `cap` ring: ✦ when ready, a counting clock after.
- **Into the Wound, composed pair** (Tendon Cut + Into the Wound): every hit slows the
  target 15% until the next swing (the Control lean); the Dagger's own poison still makes
  the target afflicted (13 per hit, no lean bleed); the firing **roots the target 2 s**
  (`payoff.kind = root`). The sentence: "A hit on a target you have already afflicted
  (every 5 s at most) roots the target for 2 s." Its moment half is Affliction green
  (`momentColor`), its payload half Control blue (`payloadColor`).
- **Two Mire Oozes** (`bog-slime`): **300 HP** each, attack **10** every **2000 ms**,
  colour `#558833`. The Slinger kills Ooze A, then turns to Ooze B. Both oozes hit the
  Slinger.
- The **floating word** is the card's name with "!" in its lane colour, rising 28 px over
  900 ms (`rhythm.floatingWord`, `compositionsT1Slinger[].floatingWord`). A thesis may
  keep it, limit it or drop it; NOTES.md says which.

## Scene 1 — Finding the tree

**Question:** where does the player go to see their cards?

- **State:** the scene-2 state above, out of combat, full screen of the game with the
  HUD, standing in Blackwater Crown, HP 171 / 193. The guide card shows the Slinger's
  line for this moment: `tutorialBeats[id=card:dagger-t1-affliction-payoff].say` ("Buy
  Into the Wound. …").
- **Must show:** the HUD as the thesis has it out of combat; the entry point to the Gear
  tab (desktop: a menu or tab bar; portrait: the bottom bar or menu, thumb-reachable);
  the Gear tab listing the four worn items, the weapon first, the three plain items
  marked as plain and leading nowhere; a press on the Poison Dagger opens the weapon page,
  which is scene 2.
- **Interaction:** HUD → Gear tab → press the Poison Dagger → the page.
- **Key frame for the fact count:** the Gear tab open, before the weapon is pressed.

## Scene 2 — The weapon page

**Question:** what does the page say about the Poison Dagger at Tier 1?

- **State:** `daggerScenes.pages[key=scene2]`. Coated Edge active; Tendon Cut buyable;
  Silent Step short (Bone 2 of 3); Into the Wound buyable (95 Rot ≥ 90, 9 Venom ≥ 8);
  Easy Prey and Next Throat short, each with its one caveat ("needs a slow", "weak on
  bosses"); the Tier 2 band locked (three rows); the gate: the Grave Toadeater, 0 of 3
  capstones, the Venom Knife, blocked ("Requires Swamp level 6.").
- **Must show:** the form header (art, name, eyebrow, meta, three lane chips); the lean
  row; the verb row; the locked Tier 2 rows; the gate block; the materials (wallet and
  where each shard drops, `page.materials[].where`); the stat sheet. Real sentences.
- **Interactions:**
  - Press **Into the Wound** → it is bought and active: render
    `pages[key=scene2-after-buy]`, toast `toasts.buyVerb`.
  - Press a **short** card → its `reason` as a toast ("Not enough Bone Shards. Shards
    drop where the map says.").
  - **Lean preview:** hovering (desktop) or long-pressing / tapping a preview control
    (portrait) a lean shows `page.leanHover[leanId]` on the stat sheet and re-reads every
    verb's sentence with that lean (`compositionsT1Slinger`, matching `leanId`). For the
    Slinger nothing on the sheet moves for any lean (its note says so): show that honestly.
- **Lean ranks:** your thesis decides. If you show ranks (mockup rule 1: three ranks, so
  Tier 1 has six steps: form, lean I–III, verb, gate), every rank number and cost is
  `[n]` and NOTES.md says you assume ranks. If you do not, NOTES.md says how six steps per
  tier would read on your page.
- **Key frame:** the page as it opens, nothing hovered.

## Scene 3 — A fight: the verb about to fire, then firing

**Question:** where do three facts live: **the verb is coming**, **the verb landed**, and
**the lasting state on the target**?

- **3a, the pure pair:** Coated Edge + Into the Wound, on two Mire Oozes, by the combat
  rule above. Sentence: "Hitting an afflicted target doubles its affliction."
- **3b, the composed pair:** Tendon Cut (Control lean) + Into the Wound
  (`pages[key=scene3-composed]`), same fight. The **two-colour sentence** (Affliction
  moment, Control payload) must be visible somewhere in your design, in or out of combat.
- **Must show, for each:** the fight with both oozes and the Slinger; HP 171 / 193 and
  falling under ooze hits; whatever your thesis uses to say the verb is coming; the body
  fx of the firing (re-dose: the poison visibly surges on the target; root: the target
  visibly pinned for 2 s); the target's badges (3a: one Poison badge, owed number jumps on
  the firing; 3b: Poison plus the slow and the root, with the existing icons).
- **Interaction:** "Land a hit" (and an auto-play toggle), a pure / composed switch.
- **Key frames for the fact count:** *coming*: the half-second before the clock clears,
  the current target afflicted; *landed*: the firing hit; *lasting*: one second after.
  Count each.

- **Must show:** firing **#1**, firing **#3**, and firing **#4** (after the teaching has

- **Interaction:** "Fire again" steps through the firings; a counter shows which firing
  this is.
- **Key frames:** firing #1 and firing #4. Count each.

## NOTES.md (beside your index.html)

1. **The thesis** in one paragraph.
2. **What you would change in the client to ship it:** files, not code (start from
   `client/src/ui/lineage/LineageTreePanel.tsx`, `weaponPageModel.ts`, `weaponPage.css`,
   `client/src/hud/RhythmReadout.tsx`, `rhythmReadoutModel.ts`, `BuffBar.tsx`,
   `targetStatusMeta.ts`, `statusHelp.ts`, `client/src/fx/cardWord.ts`,
   `client/src/ui/crafting/MakeTab.tsx`, `client/src/hud/MobileHUD.tsx`, and any other
   file you would touch, named).
3. **What the thesis cannot show well.**
4. **Card-related facts on screen, per scene and key frame**, as a table. A card-related
   fact is one piece of information about the lineage cards, their materials or their
   effects: a card name, a sentence, a card state, a cost chip, a requirement, a ring or
   strip state, a floating word, a target badge a card caused, a shard name or count, a
   "where it drops" line, a teaching line. **Count every occurrence**: the same fact shown
   in two places counts two, because duplication is what we are measuring. List them, then
   the total.
5. **Assumptions** that are the designer's to make: the one-DoT rule (everyone), lean ranks
   (if shown), the crossroads node (if shown), anything else.
6. **Placeholders**: every `[n]` and what it stands for.
7. **Readable with the sound off?** per scene: yes / partly / no, and why.

## The mockup's four rules (digest of the designer's starting point)

The legibility mockup (https://claude.ai/artifact/2z1c6vx4hM17Djt7iLAjQY), "One channel
per fact". Its card names and numbers are placeholders; use the export's.

1. **The trees live in a tab, and the lean has ranks.** The Upgrade tab's slot becomes
   Gear: the worn items, the weapon first; plain items say so and go nowhere; the weapon
   opens its page. The lean gains three ranks paid in the node's shard, so Tier 1 has six
   steps again (form, lean I–III, verb, gate) and the sentence's number grows visibly.
2. **The information budget.** Each fact has one channel. The ring says a verb is coming.
   The body shows it landing (the second slash, the lunge, the pips). The buff bar keeps
   the lasting state with a real icon. The top-left proc icons go. The callout plays for
   the first three firings of a newly bought card, as teaching, then never again.
3. **A weapon that already afflicts is amplified, not joined.** If the weapon owns a DoT
   reservoir, the Affliction lean raises that reservoir's potency and the verb re-doses
   that reservoir. Only a weapon with no DoT gets a seeded bleed, and then one id, one
   icon. The target shows one badge with one number: damage still owed.
4. **A node is a roster, not a proportion.** A node fields one family and at most one
   guest; one node per biome is the crossroads that fields all three; the node looks like
   what it pays. Open: whether the crossroads pays one shard or rolls per kill.
