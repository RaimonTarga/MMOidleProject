## Direction change

Test the candidate's economic promises before paying for a large pacing campaign. The old exhaustive sweep tested whether old content was dominant under the previous economy. V2 already passes static comparisons; the main unresolved risks are survival, ecology, overkill, colour/family routing, competing purchases, and the difference between funding an upgrade and unlocking it.

1. Qualify instruments and earn fresh candidate-lineage inputs as prerequisite work, separate from measured lives.
2. Run the targeted opportunity screen and a small mandatory stronger-character screen. Confirm failing/marginal comparisons on fresh independent worlds, preserving all originals.
3. Review Stage 1, then compile/freeze independent T1–T4 pacing lives, with baseline +3 funding/adoption and a separate optional +5 continuation.

This removes the five-way scheduling conflict. R runs first with two workers; after its review, P can use four qualified workers, one active life per tier. No higher tier waits for another *measured* P life to generate its entry. Input acquisition necessarily happens earlier and its cost is recorded separately.
