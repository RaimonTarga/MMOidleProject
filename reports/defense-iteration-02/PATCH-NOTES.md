# Proposed patch notes — defense iteration 02

Experimental addition to the existing, unmerged defense candidate:

- **Plaguebound Mantle:** Taking a direct damaging hit grants 2 plating for 4 seconds, stacking up to 10 plating. Additional hits refresh the duration. This restores protection against swarms while keeping its existing damage-over-time resistance and damage debt identity.
- **Grave Ward:** Retains its separate debt-focused design. Corrected obsolete source comments describing removed debt forgiveness.
- **Desert and Jungle:** No retained numerical changes this iteration. Longer opening protection and stronger evades were tested but held after mixed fresh-seed results.
- **Cores and classes:** No changes this iteration; melee durability advantages remain in place.

Tests used the other agent's revised Volcano values in both comparison arms. The Volcano changes are separately attributed in the report.
