## What this says

1. **T1 does not move** (1,800/1,800 identical to `final-w4.jsonl`). The parity result,
   30/30 kit cells inside ±5% with Slam rank I 1.2× on 600 ms, carries over unchanged.
2. **Slam at T2 is what X2 measured on 600 ms**, fight for fight: over Sweep, +12.9%
   (Striker) to +30.9% (Conduit), pooled +17.7%; over the 1.6 s rank II, +5.7% (Conduit) to
   +13.8% (Striker), pooled +11.2%. The class spread over Sweep is 18 points (25 on 1.6 s).
   Pooled wins 71% → 75%.
3. **boss5 Rerun 3.** The calls-2 code moves no boss5 fight: with Slam held at 1.6 s
   (`ap` castMs 1600), 72 of 78 rows equal W4-A's, and the six that move are the T4
   Apprentice's, i.e. the Pyromancer (224.3 → 281.0 dps, +25.3%; 5 → 4 wins). The 600 ms
   wind-up then lifts T3 by **+11.0%** and T4 by **+13.1%** (T4 wins 28 → 32 of 36). Every
   class rises; the Conduit least (T3 +2.0%, T4 +3.1%: it casts through one summon while the
   others keep hitting, so the stand-still was cheap for it), the T4 Spirit most (+31.4%,
   2 → 5 of 6 wins). Against W0 (Expose Weakness, before the pass), T3 is now +7.5% and T4
   −6.2% (W4-A: −3.2% and −20.4%). Below W0 still: the Striker and Slinger (T3 −3% and −8%,
   T4 −6% and −7%), the T4 Spirit (−20.5%, from −39.5%) and the T4 Apprentice (−18.6%; W0's
   was the pre-pass Pyromancer with its 1.5× max-burn hit). Single seed: each cell is one
   fight. Every cell: `final-calls-boss5-cells.md`; `stacking.md` "Rerun 3".
4. **The Pyromancer is no longer a loss against its frame, and barely a gain.** On the same
   Slam (1.6 s, X2's fights), the path goes 191.5 → **286.6** dps (+49.7%): −32.7% → **+0.7%**
   against the Ember Mage frame alone (284.7). As built (Slam 600 ms), **321.2** dps against
   the frame's 315.8: **+1.7%**, 45/60 wins against 44/60 (the one extra is a Caldera
   Sovereign fight, 5/10 against 4/10). Per T4 boss against the frame: −1.8% (Iron Crest)
   to +6.1% (Verdant Crown).
5. **Against the old (e56d530e) Pyromancer** (365.1 dps, Slam 1.6 s): before this call
   −47.5%; now **−21.5%** on the same Slam, and **−12.0%** as built (Slam 600 ms, which also
   lifts the frame itself by +10.9%). The rest of the old path's lead was its 1.5× max-burn
   bonus hit, which the frame owns now ("intensifies", design §22.5).
6. **It is still the weakest balanced path on a lone boss**: at 600 ms the frame +1.7%
   (Pyromancer), +5.4% (Firebrand), +17.8% (Cinder Lord). Two full stacks per hit only
   shorten the climb to the cap, which a long boss fight barely notices, and Radiant pays
   only with a second body adjacent; these fights have none. At 1.6 s the Pyromancer
   (286.6) now sits above X2's Winter Warden (283.6) and below every other dot path that
   X2 measured (the light and heavy paths were not rerun).
7. **T3 is untouched by the Pyromancer** (no T3 path is worn at T3). The package's T3
   Apprentice row rises with the wind-up alone: 126.5 → 137.4 dps (+8.6%), 28 → 30 of 70.
8. **Checks.** The `ap` castMs 1600 override reproduces the pre-§22.13 Slam exactly: the
   T4 frame-alone rows at 1600 equal X2's 60/60 (`--identity`), and boss5 at 1600 equals
   W4-A on every row the Pyromancer does not touch. **Seed caveat** (as X2's): the
   package's spawn is fixed, so 29 of the 43 Pyromancer arm × boss cells here are one fight
   repeated ten times (four of the six T4 bosses for the Pyromancer itself); arm-to-arm
   comparisons are exact, absolute numbers rest on few distinct fights.
