# W4-A — the Slam rank I two-number ladder (`damageMult` × wind-up)

Design §22.12: Slam rank I authors its `damageMult` AND its wind-up (`castMs`), solved together
on the same lab and seeds until every T1 kit cell is within ±5%. Candidates ran §3's commands
(README) on `plan-w4a.json` (regenerated on `feat/ap-slam-parity`, byte-identical to
`plan-final.json`) with the lab's `ap` override on every row,
`{"slam": {"field": "damageMult", "values": [<m>, 2.45, 2.7, 2.95], "castMs": <c>}}` (the lab
writes `castMs` into every rank; only rank I fights at T1), 30 seeds, against `baseline.jsonl`.
Bounds: `castMs` from 1600 down to Charge's 400 (a cast stays a cast, §22.9), on the 100 ms tick
grid (the lab ticks at 100 ms, so a wind-up between two multiples resolves on the later one).
The 1600 rows are W1-A's single-number ladder (`w1a-slam-*.jsonl`, old ranks II–IV, which no T1
fight reads). Tie-break among passing pairs: the longest wind-up, then the smallest worst cell.

**Committed: 1.2 on a 600 ms wind-up** — 30/30 kit cells inside ±5%, worst −4.7% (Striker,
Greatbear), kit range −4.7% … +4.6%, no kit win rate moves. `w4a.jsonl` (the authored values, no
override) is IDENTICAL to `w4a-slam-1.2-600.jsonl` (1,800/1,800, `--identity`, exit 0).

**Why 600 ms is the longest that passes.** The cast's hit is `round(attack × damageMult)`, and
the Striker, Apprentice and Conduit all carry attack 48 on these kits, so their cells move in
steps of one hit point (1/48 ≈ 0.021 of multiple). At 700 ms every hit size measured leaves one
cell outside, the Striker's Greatbear below and the Apprentice's Greatbear above, both smooth in
the hit: hit 60 (1.25) −5.32% / +4.73%, hit 61 (1.28) −5.14% / +4.93%, hit 62 (1.3) −4.96% /
+5.15%, hit 63 (1.31) −4.8% / +5.4%. At 800 ms the Striker's Behemoth stays below −5% (−6.6% to
−5.8%) while the Apprentice's Greatbear is already +7.4% at 1.4. At 600 ms hits 57 (1.18) and 58
(1.2) pass; 55 (1.15) and 59 (1.22) do not. Within hit 58 the worst cell (the Striker's Greatbear)
is fixed, so 1.2 is the smallest worst cell at 600 ms.

**What moved the spread.** Shortening the wind-up lifts every attacker far more than its standing
time alone (1.2 s less per ~11.6 s cycle): at 2.2, 1600 → 400 ms moves the Squire's Behemoth
+1.3% → +24.4% and the Striker's Toadeater −2.7% → +16.1%, the Conduit's Behemoth only +8.9% →
+13.2%. Lowering the multiple then trims everyone back; the Conduit, which casts while its summon
hits, keeps the smallest slope. The kit spread narrows from ~17 pp at 1600 ms to ~9 pp at 600 ms,
which is what lets every cell fit.
