# Defense measurement scope

The live server installs no diagnostic observer. The per-World observer records immutable numeric snapshots after existing combat callbacks; registration labels identify the defense group. It does not reorder, add, or alter combat effects. The wiring test checks Guard and ward attribution, conservation of their reductions, identical measured/unmeasured final damage, and observer removal.

Farming rows record signed callback damage reductions (negative values mean amplification), final direct-hit amounts, evades, opening-window uptime, last-hit and status samples, and death cause. The bench intercepts HP writes on its own bot, preserving the assigned values, to measure exact positive and negative HP changes. Analysis reconciles starting HP + positive changes − negative changes with ending HP. Positive changes are not assumed to be useful mitigation: they are recorded separately. Longer-lived characters can take and heal more total damage.

The initial baseline and Desert arm began before adding a journal observer. Later rows also contain authoritative nominal damage totals by damage type, including debt/DoTs, plus absorbed totals. These logged damage amounts can include overkill; they are not equated with actual HP removed. They are not present in the first baseline/Desert screen, and comparisons must not treat missing channels as zero. The observer forwards every journal event unchanged.

The final diagnostic hook also records primary monster-hit gross damage, post-plating damage, post-DR damage and final rounding/minimum-hit adjustment directly at the authoritative calculation. A charged-hit test checks gross 80, plating 15, post-plating 65, post-DR 48.75, rounded 49. The eight diagnostic-replay rows include this extension and reproduce their original baseline outcomes, elapsed times, kills, ending HP, HP gains/losses and direct-hit totals exactly. Earlier screening/fresh rows do not contain these additional base-layer fields.

Limitations: base-layer attribution currently covers primary monster hits, not every AoE/hazard/DoT formula. Direct-hit debt conversion measures damage deferred, not permanently prevented; debt repayment is a separate logged channel. Reactive plating works before the callbacks, so a zero callback delta does not mean it is worthless. Heal-producing callbacks can likewise have zero direct damage delta. Existing logged mitigation breakdowns are not used as a complete gross-damage ledger because their charged-hit gross basis needs separate review. No production per-layer telemetry was added.

Fixed-threat rows use the canonical defensive callbacks and timer/recovery/debt updates with explicit equal-DPS direct-hit streams: T3 30 every 500 ms versus 120 every 2000 ms; T4 scales both by 1.6. There are no autonomous player attacks, kill rewards, movement, summoned cover, automatic ability casts or environmental hazards. The attacker is a pipeline fixture, not an encounter recreation. These 60-second capped stress tests compare each change against the identical loadout and threat stream, including +0/+3/+5 gear. Their deaths are not expected farming mortality or boss difficulty.

No core values change in the three experimental armor variants. Desert DR adds six percentage points at T3/T4; Jungle frequency adds ten raw evasion points before diminishing returns; Jungle strength adds five mitigation points. Variants are isolated ITEM_DATABASE overrides captured in manifests, not edits to production recipe data. Eight declared seeds are available in the runner: four screening seeds and four fresh seeds; only completed runs count as evidence.
