## Findings

1. **A removes the demonstrated false shield refresh while retaining real resets.** In the Conduit 101003 pair, control recorded 94 `shield-session` events and 93 pre-clear events; candidate recorded 4 `shield-session` events, 70 `aggro-handover` events, and 4 pre-clear events. In the Conduit 101009 pair, the corresponding shield-session counts were 67 versus 6, with 73 candidate handovers. The candidate handovers retain the prior `aggro.sinceMs`; the remaining 4/6 shield sessions are genuine new engaged sessions with aggro, not ordinary target handovers.

2. **A restores supported Conduit work without changing the Slinger control.** Conduit kills changed 0→10 for seed 101003 and 5→8 for seed 101009. Both arms reached 300 seconds with the same 371 HP terminal value, 349 minimum HP, and 126 terminal barrier; control absorbed 30,114/21,254 versus candidate 5,175/7,399, while HP damage was 0/4,606 versus 17,959/17,141. The Slinger pairs were exact outcome/work/endpoint matches: 21 kills at seed 101003 and 18 at seed 101009. This is an A efficacy signal, not a throughput target for Slinger.

3. **B Apprentice Light / Venom vessel improved in all four tested pairs.** The declared defensive-stance candidate beat the reused offensive-stance control in elapsed survival and completed work in Volcanic and Tundra. It reached the 30-minute cap in two pairs and died later than control in the other two; no opposing pair regression was observed.

4. **B Striker Heavy A / Berserker and Heavy C / Juggernaut improved in all eight tested pairs.** The Inferno Heart +5 candidate reached the cap in four of eight pairs and extended survival/work in the other four. The table preserves null post-death endpoints; lifetime kills in a death row are not late-window progress.

5. **Champion has one serious opposing counterexample.** T4 Conduit Heavy B / Champion improved in three pairs, including two cap survivors, but in Desert-03 seed 101021 the candidate died at 42.9 seconds with 2 kills versus the reused control at 88.0 seconds with 4 kills. The declared package readback trades 239 starting barrier for higher Recovery; this early failure is consistent with a barrier-for-recovery durability gap, not evidence for a global Conduit conclusion.
