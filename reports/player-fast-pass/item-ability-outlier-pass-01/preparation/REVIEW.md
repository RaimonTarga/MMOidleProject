## Mobility

**Implementation fact:** `mobilityBoots.ts::bootSpeedMultiplier` sums eligible boot percentage bonuses into 1+pct. Desert requires actual motion with a negative dot product against the direction to the current living monster target. Orbit does not imply 100% kiting uptime. Mountain requires positive dot product and center distance >64; its 125% base /145% +4 /150% +5 is a gap-closing service, not in-contact speed. Volcano's 70% passive applies unless its hit-suppression status exists; the player-defender onDamageTaken listener refreshes suppression for the authored 3500ms, without an additional positive-damage check in that listener. Tundra uses continuous moving milliseconds × .35/sec capped at .75 before upgrades; stopping resets the counter to zero (a comment saying “bleeds off” is not runtime behavior). Ideal base ramp saturation is .75/.35 = 2.143s uninterrupted movement.

Swamp soft-slow magnitude uses `1-(1-mult)*(1-min(.9,resistance))`; multipliers <=0 or >=1 pass through, so roots remain roots. Trench/Graveyard tenacity reduces movement-control duration, capped at 90%, and is a different service. Plains OOC speed and kill haste, Cave stealth, Jungle aggro pull, Graveyard kill momentum and Trench utilities retain their distinct conditions; their complete values and upgrade deltas are in mobility.tsv. No further numerical normalization is staged.

Swamp chain: `swamp-boots-t1 -> swamp-boots-t2 -> swamp-boots-t3`; no successor has `evolvesFrom=swamp-boots-t3`. Graveyard and Trench do not continue that utility. `itemEvolution.ts` consumes/replaces the predecessor and equips the new definition; it does not add predecessor passives or transfer upgrades. T3 remains legal at T4. A minimal unimplemented lineage proposal is in BACKLOG.md.

**Designer preference:** retain T1 utility and use modest mature-tier increments. D and S precisely implement the brief's proposed numbers; resolved sampled S values are T2 +4 .48, T3 +3 .56, not the +5 catalogue values. **Calculation:** isolated residual slows and speed ratios are in CANDIDATES.md. **Untested hypothesis:** these curves restrain progression without breaking escape, target engagement or overpull behavior. The 16 mobility lives address two T4 Desert-kiting packages plus established T2 melee Desert (Numbing Sting .5 multiplier/4000ms) and T3 ranged Swamp (Plague-Shell Snapper pool .7 multiplier) exposure; they do not prove every boot family/tier.

**Base-speed separation:** `shared/src/systems/stats.ts` resets speed from GAME_CONFIG.PLAYER_SPEED, folds flat item stat contributions, then server movement applies conditional multipliers. The flat boot speed column rises independently of conditional passives. No base-speed, `stats.speed`, upgrade-speed, enemy-speed or class change is included.

## Weapons, armor, recovery, cores and relics

**Implementation fact:** ordinary `runPlayerAttack` computes/commits dead-swing cadence only after beforeAttack accepts the attack; cancelled attacks do not consume it. Formation delivery uses procWeight and an owner accumulator rather than charging every body a full owner hit. On a dead swing direct damage becomes zero; the weapon's vulnerability listener still has an intentional payoff. Reload ammo and empowered charge preservation have explicit chaoticMiss guards. `formationAttack.ts` and summoner listeners consume the resulting flag. This is a meaningful cost, not grounds to flatten the Cave family.

**Important adapter boundary:** owner cast payloads in `abilityEffects.ts::resolveCastPayload` use Attack and applyPlayerAoe, explicitly bypassing ordinary attacks and their weapon proc/dead-swing counting. Devout Priest beam and Melter laser have their own ticking pipeline rather than calling runPlayerAttack, so a shared event pipeline alone does not prove dead-swing cadence is charged. Native formed ordinary attacks do pay the weighted penalty. This is an implementation fact requiring an intent decision, not a confirmed bug or a covert reason to reduce all axe Attack. No affected channel package is used to assert the axe universally pays its drawback.

Armor/recovery screen: recipes author additive stats and named conditional defenses. Mountain burst-cap/Guard support and barrier charm are not identical to sustained HP/Recovery; Volcano kill-conditioned recovery needs kills, Jungle ramp needs time, and Swamp recovery pulses need their trigger. Review consumers under `server/src/systems/defense/`, combat damage and shared stat rebuild; full fields/paths are indexed in inventory.json. Historical charm/relic contrasts provide the useful screening evidence; no wrong-unit correction or new armor/charm coefficient is justified by this breadth pass. Item evasion values are fractions in current formulas despite an old ItemStats comment describing 1/N; no gameplay change is derived from that comment.

Core/relic screen: actual eligibility is checked at materialization, not inferred from item name. Arcanist is unrestricted T3 Mountain18, +0, with no general damage/HP bonus. Tempered authors +12% general dealt damage and +12% maximum HP; composed values are in applied receipts. Accelerant authors +.55 attack speed and -.18 dealt damage; an isolated 1.55*.82=1.271 basic-attack product ignores additive speed, defense, channels and opportunity costs. Colossus changes class-mechanic frequency/potency, not ordinary attack APS. Hastebound and Equilibrium are tradeoffs, not universal throughput multipliers. Existing measured Ritualist/Melter counterexamples are retained in EVIDENCE_INDEX.md. No inverse core is authored.

## Arcanist and nominated abilities

**Implementation fact:** TECHNIQUE_POWER_FIELDS applies only to designated offensive fields: splashPct, cast/armed damageMult, reposition empowerMult, detonateMult, imbue onHitDamage. It excludes reach, control duration, cast time, Frenzy attack speed, Expose vulnerability and Contagion breadth. Guard payloads are excluded. Technique cooldown reduction applies through semantic Technique tags, adds applicable mobility/cleanse reduction and caps at .90. It is independent of cast speed. In fixed Power Strike/Frenzy kits: Power Strike receives power and cooldown; Frenzy receives cooldown only. Other inherited Techniques in the Reverb Arcanist kit have their field eligibility listed in inventory and resolved readbacks; no autonomous execution/discharge multiplier is claimed.

Power Strike: 6 RP, 1600ms cast, 10000ms base cooldown, Attack multipliers 3/3.5/4/4.5 across T1–T4. Slam: 6 RP, same windup/cooldown, 1.75/2/2.25 at T2–T4 and radius 150. At equal tier, two affected targets match Power Strike's nominal summed payload, not necessarily useful damage. Cast cooldown starts on resolution; target loss/hard control can abort without spending cooldown, while occupying the offensive channel. Summoner delivery uses formation charge/eligible bodies and normalized magnitude; it is not owner damage multiplied by minion count.

Quick Strike: 5 RP, armed empower payload delivered through ordinary/adapted attacks; opportunity and consumed charges matter more than activations. Frenzy: 6 RP, instant attack-speed buff, cooldown benefit from Arcanist but no power scaling. Fixed-rate channels cannot be assigned the ordinary-attack speed payoff by name. The two selected ability questions compare 6-RP Power Strike against 5-RP Quick Strike with 1 RP deliberately unspent, same Frenzy and defenses. This is a declared alternative allocation, not an equal-cost or pure cast-time ablation and not proof that every unused point is worthless. No timing Rune is added.

Endure: 6 RP, HP-below-.70 native trigger. T2/T3/T4 ranks are 18% DR for 8s, 20% for 8s, 20% for 10s; 14s cooldown. It remains a threat-specific long window competing with other paid guards, not a universal bargain demonstrated by catalogue frequency. No defensive removal arm is scheduled.

Contagion: 7 RP, 1000ms cast, copies owned affliction state rather than consuming it; radius/target caps scale by rank and do not receive Technique power. Detonate: 6 RP, consumes eligible owned DoT value and scales detonateMult, not the already-earned stored damage a second time. `abilityAffliction.ts` declines casts without work. Fully Afflicted is invalid on auto-consuming or uncapped stack paths; these are not interpreted as ability failures. Both were inspected but are outside the two selected combat questions.

## Five remaining Rites and Rune/stance affordability

`riteOoc.ts` runs end effects exactly once at POST_COMBAT -> OUT_OF_COMBAT. Swift Repose costs 2 RP, reduces boundary delay by 50%; Lingering Battle costs 2, adds 50%; together they cancel and the timer has a 250ms floor. Purification costs 3, removes qualifying carried harmful effects, preserves active terrain/ground-zone ownership; no inferred Heat-clearing role. Mechanic Renewal costs 5: cooldown execution remaining*.7; energy +30% max capped; reload adds ceil(.3*max ammo) and reload timer*.7; cadence adds ceil(.3*threshold) capped below trigger; summoner advances only an active reconstruction by .3*duration; DoT has no portable benefit. Do not infer special-channel or full-formation restoration from its name. Ability Reprieve costs 5 and multiplies remaining equipped Technique and Guard cooldowns by .7 once at the boundary.

Prior Reverb Tundra traces contain actual first-ten-minute OOC transitions with remaining ability cooldowns: 20/20 for seed101009 and 21/21 for seed101033. `rite-opportunity-evidence.json` stores exact times, cooldowns and raw hashes. This justifies a new bounded opportunity test, not a guarantee the modified current kit reproduces all opportunities. New observations must report delivered boundaries/remaining cooldowns and lack of useful opportunity if observed.

Starter maintenance availability is acquisition, not free RP or default equipping. Reload Safely, Ready Execution, Rebuild Formation and Recover First each cost 1 action RP. Always costs 0; Out of Combat costs 1, so assembled rules cost 1 or 2 before ability/stance attunements. Historical explicit rules are retained, with fresh current starter ownership in current receipts. No new maintenance rule is silently injected. Rebuild Formation's absence from old packages remains explicit, and its potential to change Rite marginal value is deferred.

Stances receive a compatibility screen only: every package has its one declared paid stance and no switch rule; offensive damage/mitigation, defensive durability and recuperating boundary behavior cannot be compared through nominal Attack alone. No 54-path stance search and no repricing. Rides were not assessed.
