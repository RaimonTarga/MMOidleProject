# Proposed T4 coverage — catalogue only, no launch authority

Exactly 54 current production specialization keys match the selected catalogue: six roots x three frames x three paths. Reconstruction treatment rows and range alternatives are not additional identities. `t4-catalogue.json` records each production name, skill path, authored mechanic/description, range exception and historical build direction; those historical equipment lists are guidance, not qualified new packages.

## Proposed scope to bring to the command center

54 identities x four contexts x seeds 101009 and 101033 = **432 observations**, ten-minute ordinary farming and five-minute bosses. Proposed fixtures: Volcano `node-t4-volcanic-01`, Tundra `node-t4-tundra-01`, Mountain boss `node-t4-mountain-dungeon` / `iron-crest-titan`, Wasteland boss `node-t4-graveyard-dungeon` / `charnel-crown-sovereign`. Wasteland's production biome key is `graveyard` (displayNames.ts); do not invent a `wasteland` fixture. Boss access remains a separately labelled synthetic setup, not guardian progression evidence.

Proposed established checkpoint: **T4 +4, GM146 / 45 RP**. Mastery: Plains/Forest12; Swamp/Cave18; Mountain22; Jungle/Desert18; Volcano/Tundra12; Wasteland4; Trench0. These are within current production caps. This is established T4 after Mountain/Wasteland progress, not entry T4 and not the mature GM156 +5 catalogue with reduced item numbers. Fixed per-slot upgrade ceilings, lawful prior paid ownership and actual composed RP must be qualified before launch.

At this proposed checkpoint the catalogue's ordinary T4 items fit the inspected acquisition/+4 gates, including Mountain gear at level22 and Plague Axe at Wasteland4. Catalyst core (Volcano10) and Hastebound Dial (Volcano11) fit by prior ownership; non-upgradeable supports remain +0, not +4. Equilibrium Shard and Colossus Heart require Mountain24 and are **unavailable**. Proposed main packages omit those two relics; retain legal earlier support or no relic, documenting the resulting cadence/potency tradeoff. Do not silently raise Mountain to24. Hastebound is only a proposed path-specific legal option, not a free replacement everywhere. Powering Up stance requires Trench2 and is unavailable at Trench0; do not grant it. Other stances require their actual recipe gates and RP, not a T4 label.

## Build directions and expression checks

Use one credible package per identity/context, not a weapon/core/relic grid. Volcano prioritizes delivered AoE, appropriate recovery and actual Heat exposure/escape. Tundra needs durable or kiting delivery against sparse elites/control. Mountain needs single-target work and real charge/impact response. Wasteland needs recurrent-add handling plus boss pressure; a pure boss-only damage package is insufficient.

The current catalogue supplies path-specific fast on-hit weapons, high-Attack payload weapons, empowered weapons, support cores, and native summon formations. Preserve those mechanisms while recomputing the 45-RP budget with the legal supports above. Keep close-range exceptions for Blunderbuss (`reload-balanced-t3-b`) and Champion (`summoner-heavy-t3-b`); use the exact production names below as authoritative. Channel paths must not be neutralized by incidental Orbit, while telegraph escape remains. Charge paths need time and targeting to complete charges; DoT paths need owned stacking targets; Conduit needs its authored formation, replacement costs and owner contribution. An equipped but unexpressed specialization is a flagged fixture limitation, not proof of class weakness.

The table gives proposed equipment directions from the existing catalogue. It is not a sealed loadout or a certification of optimality. Ordered techniques/Guards/rules, costs, mastery receipts, mechanic-expression checks and any support substitution still require the final preparation pass and command-center approval. No T4 pilot or combat was performed here.

## Approval and stopping rule

Ask the command center to approve the explicit GM146/45-RP established checkpoint, four resolved contexts, seeds and support directions (especially omission of Mountain24 relics), or name a specific replacement checkpoint. Final full packages and known path-specific limitations must be approved before launch. First Trench/Wasteland entry is a separate later small +3/+4 contrast only if it needs a decision; no automatic second grid.

Reuse historical breadth evidence with its own source/gear/strategy labels. Old mature +5, pre-fix, or pre-Spirit-patch outcomes are historical context, never current measurements. Keep the present Spirit inheritance and framed Conduit baseline.

Close the class pass only after concentrated T2/T3 failures are dispositioned, every selectable T4 path has credible farm/boss coverage, persistent outliers receive only approved corrections and focused regressions, and the root/frame/path disposition map plus patch ledger records remaining watch items. Asymmetry and imperfect credible builds are acceptable. Untouched paths or serious unexplained progression failures are not certification. No arbitrary buff/nerf quota or exhaustive Runic-build search.
