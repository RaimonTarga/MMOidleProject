## Jungle ordinary

Light Striker died at 129.2s after 11 kills; Balanced Apprentice at 368.2s after 27. Sampled attacking/chasing crowds peaked at 3 and 4. Striker's last three samples had three engaged enemies; two distinct Silverbacks and a Chameleon delivered the final direct-hit sequence. HP fell from 200.65 at 127s to 2.94 at 129s. Apprentice's last samples had 3/3/4 engaged enemies; two Stalkers plus a Chameleon supplied the last hits, alongside deferred debt. Silverback supplied the largest lifetime direct-hit contribution in both lives: 1,989 and 3,108.8 HP damage. Final killer alone would conceal this exposure.

The intended AoE/avoidance response was partially expressed: 34/85 Sweep activations and footprints; Avoid Hazards enabled; Apprentice recorded 12 hazard-escape events and had Orbit selected. Both had legal, attuned Cleanse, but no Cleanse activation; recorded incoming damage was direct plus **deferred debt**, not a poison/fire DoT stream. Debt is the production hit-to-DoT pool, not proof that Cleanse missed a removable poison. Second Wind fired 5/11 times. No static hazard-contact samples were present. A selected Orbit intent did not guarantee displacement: the Apprentice's last three sampled motions were absent.

Both wore Swamp armor +3, whereas the proposed starting answer emphasizes plating. This is a package discrepancy, not proof that an armor substitution would win. AoE footprints show delivery geometry, not the number of useful secondary hits; the final samples show continued engagement rather than a successful recovery break. Hypothesis: overlapping direct-hit pressure and incomplete disengagement exhaust these packages despite their AoE. These traces do not isolate armor, root, or navigation causally.

## Volcano ordinary

Light Striker died at 16.7s after 3 kills; Balanced Apprentice at 76.1s after 14. Sampled crowds peaked at 4/9, with 2/5 engaged in the last sample. Striker took 823 recorded HP damage, all direct: Ash Salamander 366, Cinder Hound 294, Ember Scuttler 163. Apprentice took 1,823.7 direct plus 172 deferred-debt damage. Neither selected life recorded incoming fire DoT or static lava-contact damage. Therefore neither is a demonstrated fire-DoT or lava death.

Heat reached 5/8 stacks near the end, displaying +22.5%/+36% damage taken and +15%/+24% damage dealt. Heat is an exposure amplifier, not a separate HP-damage event to subtract from the total. The final Apprentice tick combined two 21.6 Scuttler hits and a 68.4 Hound hit, following HP of 238.98/98.78/93.78 at 74/75/76s. Avoid Hazards was selected; that does not remove ordinary direct-hit pressure or certify disengagement. Sweep fired 5/16 times, Second Wind 1/5, Brace 1/5. Recovery did not prevent the terminal burst.

Cleanse was legally purchasable (Swamp mastery 18, recipe gate 3) but not learned/attuned in these packages. Its omission is **not demonstrated as the cause**: the frozen `statusPolicy.ts` makes Heat immune, and the observed debt pool is not an ordinary cleanseable status. No recorded removable fire DoT establishes a missed Cleanse rescue. Volcano mastery was zero: Volcanic charm/Frenzy were unavailable; neither may be assumed as the arrival answer.

## T2 Plains bosses

Both selected Light melee lives died at 9.7s. Two Hares appeared at 7.9s after Rallying Cry, but dealt only 2 HP total to each player; the boss dealt 335/365. Boss HP remaining was 2,851/3,237 of 4,000. This is not evidence of lethal add damage.

Striker armed Sweep four times and delivered four footprints, kept all 15 ordinary player hits on the boss, and killed no adds. Squire cast Slam at 1.4s and completed it at 2.8s, **before** the adds appeared. Its ordinary target changed to a Hare at 9.5s, killing one 0.2s before death. Both used Second Wind at 3.5s; neither package attuned Brace. The intended AoE plus add-supported sustain therefore existed on the loadout but barely operated during the add window. Striker had no kill to start kill recovery; Squire's single late kill left only 0.2s for recovery. Existing heal events do not exclusively attribute healing to the charm, so no exact charm-heal amount is claimed. The production on-kill listener activates a finite recovery window rather than instant full healing.

The strategy is established; a timing/target-delivery audit is supported before proposing enemy changes. These traces do not establish a broken Sweep or Slam implementation.

## Three priorities for the designer

1. **Jungle:** assess the known plating/AoE/avoidance strategy against the actual Swamp-armor package and final multi-attacker engagement. A separately authorized package/delivery review is supported; an armor sweep or enemy nerf is not supported by these two lives alone.
2. **Ordinary Volcano and Plains:** keep damage source and response timing separate. These Volcano deaths do not justify a Heat or fire-DoT nerf; Plains warrants reviewing whether AoE/add kills arrive early enough to provide sustain before boss pressure wins. No loadout changes are bundled here.
3. **Apprentice bosses:** retain the late Mountain charge failure and Volcano finisher/Guard timing as distinct counterplay questions. Before any new strategy packet, specify the exact intended Execute behavior/skill and decide whether to test legally purchased Detonate. Its performance and any revised timing are untested. This review does not delay the separate S/C decisions after the 56-case comparison.
