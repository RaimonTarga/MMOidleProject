## 1. Questions and designer decisions

The overnight campaign should establish:

1. Whether Conduit's low useful output already exists at T1, before frames, and whether that pattern persists across contrasting content and development stages.
2. Whether Spirit's strong farming packages remain broadly strong with legal progression equipment, and how its frames perform against bosses. Heavy's large, less-frequent discharge being better on durable targets is a hypothesis, not proven overkill attribution.
3. Whether legal arrival packages can establish themselves in Jungle, Desert, Volcano, and Tundra, without tools earned only after mastering that destination.
4. Which persistent weak/strong outliers warrant a targeted adjustment, versus healthy specialization or a limited known matchup.

**The designer's newest build guidance takes priority:**

- At T1, use **Chaotic Axe** as the primary weapon for Striker, Apprentice, Slinger, Conduit, and Spirit. Squire uses **Heavy Hammer**. The designer considers the axe generally best and Flash Rapier the main alternative for fast-attack roots, including Slinger. Treat that ranking as the experienced starting hypothesis, not a result this campaign has already established.
- T2 Desert melee entry is deliberately a **tank-the-dealer** stage before the targeting unlock. Use substantial mitigation, Brace, Mountain armor, a high-output weapon, and real sustain. Striker's starting weapon is the same-tier Cave axe, **Ruinous Axe**; Squire's is **Quake Hammer**. This resolves the informal “Chaotic Axe / Mountain hammer” into the T2 lineages; it is not a new weapon search.
- Test the **Cave charm** as an explicit damage-received recovery alternative in Desert. Mountain approach boots are the fixed melee default for that comparison; Swamp slow-resistance boots are an approved preparation option, not a second silently changed treatment.
- After its actual unlock, low-HP targeting is an available Desert solution, not a free first-entry ability or mandatory answer for every class. Ranged separation/kiting remains legitimate.

**Ask before inventing:** use all the designer's existing biome/boss strategies. Resolve routine catalogue and costing details without another approval loop. Do not seek approval again for the tanking strategy just supplied. A gameplay loss does not stop the rest of the approved ledger.
